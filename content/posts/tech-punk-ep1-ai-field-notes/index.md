In Episode 1 of Tech Punk, I share my views and questions about AI, covering personal and widespread sentiment, making predictions on the future of AI and whether the bubble will burst, and dissecting the issues of AI.

While I give myself the freedom to diverge, most of the episodes are meant to be on topics like digital freedom, privacy, technology, sovereignty, independent thought, decentralization, institutions, infrastructure, information systems, open source, tech history, and software engineering.
